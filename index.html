Bay City, Michigan

Christopher Runberg

HDIS Sales Consultant  ·  City Commissioner, Ward 7

I've served as a Cavalry Scout in Iraq, spent over a decade in telecom (the last four years leading a team of network engineers), and worked my way from store associate to sales consultant at The Home Depot, where I help homeowners make confident decisions about their windows and doors. Since 2024, I've served my neighbors as Bay City's Ward 7 City Commissioner. Wherever I've worked, I've believed in the same things: clear communication, follow-through, and showing up for the people I serve.

What I Believe

My North Star is leaving the world better than I found it. If I can say I did that, and did it my way, I'll have nothing to regret.

I've survived war, addiction, and two suicide attempts. I walked away from them without a fear of death. What I fear instead are the thoughts I might hold on my deathbed, so I try to live in a way I won't have to make peace with later.

I won't compromise on human dignity, compassion, or empathy. Every conscious being deserves opportunity, and I want my actions to serve human flourishing. Curiosity and honesty about my own experience are the values I hold highest.

I'm not sure humanity has many generations left. But as we create new intelligences in our own image, I believe our purpose is to give them moral foundations worth carrying forward, perhaps across the cosmos. Consciousness, I believe, may be how the universe comes to know itself.

More than anything, I hope people leave knowing they met someone genuine.
